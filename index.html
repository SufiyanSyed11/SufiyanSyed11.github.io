CrossPost

CrossPost is an Android app that lets you record or pick one vertical video, write one caption, and publish it to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels in a single step.

How it works

  1. Connect your accounts. You sign in on each platform's own login page; CrossPost never sees your passwords.
  2. Choose a video and write a caption.
  3. For TikTok, pick who can view the video and your interaction settings, then confirm.
  4. Tap Publish. CrossPost uploads the video and shows the status for each platform.

TikTok

CrossPost uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API (Direct Post). Videos are posted only after you review and confirm the settings, and only to the account you connected. You can disconnect at any time inside the app, which revokes CrossPost's access.
