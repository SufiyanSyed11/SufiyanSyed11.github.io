Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

CrossPost Studio is operated by Sufiyan Syed. CrossPost Studio has no server of its own that stores your data: it runs on your phone and talks directly to TikTok, YouTube and Instagram.

Data we handle

  • Account connection data. When you connect an account we receive an access token and refresh token from the platform, plus your display name and avatar (for TikTok, via the user.info.basic scope). These are stored only on your device.
  • Videos and captions. The video you pick and the caption you write are read from your device and sent to the platforms you choose to publish to. We do not keep copies on any server.
  • TikTok creator information. To show the correct posting options, the app asks TikTok for your nickname, available privacy levels and posting limits when you open the post screen.

What we do not do

We do not see your passwords, sell or share your data, run advertising or analytics trackers, or send your data to any server other than the platform APIs you use.

How the data is used

Only to sign you in, show your connected accounts, and publish the content you choose, when you choose.

Retention and deletion

Tokens and settings stay on your device until you disconnect the account in the app (which also asks TikTok to revoke the token) or uninstall the app. To remove access from the platform side, use TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services), your Google Account permissions page, or Instagram's Apps and websites settings. Videos already published remain on the platform until you delete them there.

Third parties

Data you send to TikTok, Google or Meta is governed by their privacy policies.

Children

CrossPost Studio is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
